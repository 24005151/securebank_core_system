Roles & Permissions

sysadmin

Full access — manage staff, purge audit logs, all manager actions.

manager

Create/edit/delete customers, manage transactions, export CSV, view all data.

staff

View customers and transactions. Cannot create, edit, or delete records.

Managing Customers

  • Use the Dashboard to create new customers and view the full list.
  • Click View on a customer row to open their full profile — transactions, balance chart, notes, and timeline.
  • Click Edit to update name, email, or notes.
  • Use Deactivate / Activate to suspend or reinstate accounts without deleting data.
  • The Export CSV button in the customer profile downloads all their transactions.
  • Customers with a balance below £2.50 are flagged as low balance.

Transactions

  • Deposit — adds funds to a customer account.
  • Withdraw — removes funds from a customer account.
  • Transfer — moves funds between two customer accounts.
  • Transactions of £10 or more are automatically risk-flagged and appear in Alerts.
  • Use the Transactions page to filter by date range, type, and amount.

Audit Log

  • Every action — login, customer change, transaction, export — is recorded automatically.
  • Use the Audit Log page to filter by actor, event type, result, or date range.
  • Sysadmins can purge entries older than a chosen number of days.
  • Audit logs cannot be edited — they are append-only.

Staff Management

  • Only managers and sysadmins can access the Staff page.
  • Accounts are automatically locked after 3 consecutive failed login attempts.
  • Locked accounts are shown with a red badge and sorted to the top of the list.
  • Use the Unlock button to restore access.
  • New staff are created with a temporary password and must change it on first login.

Exports & Reports

  • Export all customers or all transactions as CSV from the Dashboard.
  • Export a single customer's transactions using the Export CSV button in their profile.
  • The Reports page shows monthly transaction volumes, top customers, and type breakdowns.
  • All exports are logged in the audit trail.

Security

  • Sessions expire after 20 minutes of inactivity. A warning appears at 18 minutes.
  • All passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256.
  • CSRF tokens protect every state-changing request.
  • Content-Security-Policy headers prevent XSS and clickjacking.
  • Change your password any time from Settings.

Tips

  • Use the Dark mode button in the footer to switch themes. Preference is saved automatically.
  • The customer search box on the Dashboard supports live suggestions as you type.
  • The Customer Detail section has its own search to quickly find any customer by name or account number.
  • Balance history charts are generated automatically when you view a customer with transactions.
